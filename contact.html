Do I need an appointment to drop off consignment pieces? ▾

Walk-ins are welcomed during standard salon hours. For collections with 5+ pieces or rare exotics, we recommend booking a private appraisal slot in advance.

Is private valet parking available? ▾

Yes, complimentary underground secure valet parking is available for clients at our Paris Place Vendome and London Mayfair salons.

Our on-site master authenticators complete preliminary physical audits on the spot, with full spectral certificates generated within 24 hours.
